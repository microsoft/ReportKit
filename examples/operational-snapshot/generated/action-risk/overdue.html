Queue definitions

Closed, completed and cancelled lifecycle records are excluded from action queues. An explicit open lifecycle remains actionable regardless of health status. When lifecycle is missing or unknown, attention uses warning, critical, blocked, failed, in-progress, pending-review and not-started statuses, or an explicit blocker. Overdue is strictly before the generated UTC date; due next seven days includes that date through seven days later. Queues may overlap; counts are not additive. Ordering: priority, explicit blocker first, due date, then ID.
